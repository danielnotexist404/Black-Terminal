# BCLIF Thermal Presentation V9

## Objective

V9 restores the operational visual language of a professional liquidation map: a continuous deep-plasma field, crisp horizontal liquidity shelves, full plot coverage, and a selectable proprietary Black Terminal palette. The presentation is intentionally separated from BCLIF model authority.

## Themes

### Purple Plasma · CoinGlass

The complete plot receives a deep purple plasma underlay. Valid low-intensity context progresses through violet and blue, then cyan and green. Only the strongest relative display cores reach lime or yellow. The palette is continuous, so no transparent or black side void can appear when the model window is shorter than the chart.

### Blood / White / Silver · Black Terminal

The same causal intensity field maps to near-black, blood red, crimson, silver, and white. Theme selection changes only presentation bytes and the display-raster identity. It does not change exposure, evidence, confidence, or shelf locations.

## Shelf construction

The display worker projects the immutable model into a high-DPI raster capped at 1,536 time columns. It selects a bounded number of distinct price-local peaks, snaps only their presentation centers to a small high-resolution price quantum, and carries them from past to present while display mass remains. Bounded halos provide thermal legibility without merging the full chart into one cloud.

Half-mitigated visibility is controlled by the residual shelf setting. Lower values remove exhausted bands faster; higher values keep weak remaining mass visible longer. Zero validity or zero alpha always terminates a shelf immediately. The pass is causal: a future activation cannot repaint the historical prefix.

## Controls

- Thermal Theme: Purple Plasma or Blood / White / Silver.
- Heatmap Opacity: texture visibility.
- Plasma Background: plot-wide underlay strength.
- Thermal Color Floor: minimum valid thermal color.
- Shelf Clarity: peak contrast and activation threshold.
- Residual / Half-Mitigated Shelves: weak remaining-mass visibility.
- Gamma, robust bounds, smoothing, and channel opacity: existing display-transfer controls.

Defaults are tuned for a crisp operational field at desktop and high-DPI tablet resolutions. Settings schema V9 migrates older Reference Thermal workspaces to these defaults.

## Truth contract

The underlay is explicitly presentation background, not exposure. Shelf colors describe relative modeled intensity. Evidence authorization stays in the separate confidence, source, provenance, and yellow-eligibility channels. Browser fallback remains labeled persistence off and cannot acquire exact-event authority through palette selection, contrast, or shelf persistence.
