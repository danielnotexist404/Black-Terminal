# BCLIF Calibration

Calibration stores immutable prediction/outcome pairs with original model/rules/prior/confidence, later observed overlap, price/timing error, absorption/continuation, and sample count. Reported targets include hit/miss/false-positive rates, mean/median price error, timing error, confidence error, cascade precision/recall, and absorption accuracy. Deterministic topology tests are not market calibration. Production calibration remains pending until the collector accumulates sufficient observed liquidation, trade, OI, and book history; no mature score is claimed.
